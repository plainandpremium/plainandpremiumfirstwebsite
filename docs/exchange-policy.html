Exchanges

We offer exchanges for size or preference changes within 7 days of delivery. We do not accept change-of-mind returns or provide refunds for them.

Eligibility and charges

  • Your T-shirt must be unworn, unwashed and in its original condition, with the tags and original packaging intact.
  • Please keep your order confirmation or proof of purchase.
  • A ₹50 exchange shipping charge applies to each approved size or preference exchange.
  • Exchanges are subject to stock availability and inspection of the item.

How to request an exchange

Within 7 days of delivery, WhatsApp +91 9928109868 with your order number, the item you purchased and the replacement size you need. Our team will confirm availability and provide payment and shipping instructions.

Please wait for our approval before sending an item back. Once we receive and inspect it, we will arrange the approved exchange. Exchange requests are handled directly by our support team.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

Please inspect your order on arrival. If an item is damaged, defective or incorrect, contact us promptly with your order number and photos so we can review and resolve the issue. The change-of-mind restrictions above do not limit your rights under applicable consumer law.
